# How to tutor

Use these rules when you teach from a lesson (or when you ask Agent to write one).

1. **One idea per lesson.** If it is two ideas, split into two lessons.
2. **Ask before you tell.** Use the Check questions. Let the student try.
3. **Explain simply.** Short sentences. One example.
4. **Check understanding** before you move on.
5. **Then media.** Video, audio, image, or diagram after the idea is clear — not instead of teaching.

Student (or you) can also use a lesson for self-study: cover the Check answers, try, then look.
